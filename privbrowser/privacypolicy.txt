PRIVBROWSER PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: September 22, 2026

PrivBrowser is an open-source desktop web browser developed by Andar Studio.

PrivBrowser is designed to minimize unnecessary data collection by the application itself.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

PrivBrowser does not require users to create a PrivBrowser account.

Depending on how the browser is used, information may be stored locally on the user's device, including:

- Browsing history
- Cookies
- Website data
- Cached files
- Saved sessions
- Browser settings
- Download information

This information is generally stored locally on the user's device and is not intentionally sent to Andar Studio.

2. THIRD-PARTY WEBSITES

Websites accessed through PrivBrowser may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

This may include:

- IP address
- Browser and operating-system information
- Cookies
- Local storage
- Information submitted by the user
- Network or device information

Andar Studio does not control the privacy practices of third-party websites.

3. COOKIES AND WEBSITE DATA

Websites may use cookies, local storage, cache, and similar technologies.

PrivBrowser may store this information locally to allow websites to function correctly.

4. PRIVACY FEATURES

PrivBrowser may provide privacy and tracking-protection features.

These features do not guarantee complete anonymity, privacy, or security.

Users should not assume that PrivBrowser makes them completely anonymous online.

5. ANALYTICS AND TELEMETRY

PrivBrowser does not intentionally collect general-purpose analytics or tracking data unless such functionality is explicitly introduced and documented.

If telemetry, analytics, crash reporting, or similar functionality is introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated.

6. DOWNLOADS AND THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Downloads made through PrivBrowser are stored according to the browser and operating-system settings.

Andar Studio is not responsible for the privacy, security, or content of third-party websites, services, extensions, or downloaded files.
