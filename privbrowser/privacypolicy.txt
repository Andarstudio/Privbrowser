# Privacy Policy for PrivBrowser

**Effective Date:** September 24, 2026

**Developer:** Andar Studio

**Repository:** [https://github.com/Andarstudio/Privbrowser](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/Andarstudio/Privbrowser&utm_source=gemini)

---

### 1. Overview

PrivBrowser is built from the ground up with a local-first, privacy-focused design. Developed by Andar Studio, PrivBrowser operates under a strict principle: **your personal data stays on your device and remains under your control.**

### 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Andar Studio does not collect, track, store, or transmit any of your personal data or usage activity. Specifically:

* **No Telemetry or Usage Tracking:** We do not track application launches, feature usage, crash reports, or hardware identification.
* **No Cloud Accounts or Central Servers:** PrivBrowser does not use external user account databases, authentication servers, or sync services managed by Andar Studio.
* **No Remote Browsing Logs:** Your URLs, search queries, page titles, and session details are never sent to external servers owned by Andar Studio.

### 3. Data Stored Locally on Your Machine

PrivBrowser saves certain data locally to your computer solely to provide standard browser capabilities:

* **Browsing History & Bookmarks:** Stored in local application storage files on your device.
* **Password Vault & Saved Credentials:** Saved credentials and password manager entries reside strictly in local encrypted files on your disk.
* **Profiles & Custom Preferences:** Custom user configurations, layout settings, and active profiles are saved locally.
* **Terminal Command History:** Logs and outputs executed inside the internal terminal control remain strictly in memory or local application storage.

You can wipe your browsing history, saved credentials, and profile storage at any time via the browser settings or terminal interface.

### 4. Ad & Tracker Protection

PrivBrowser uses built-in network request filters to detect and block third-party trackers, telemetry scripts, and advertisement hosts before connections are initialized.

* Tracking request filtration happens entirely on your machine.
* No outbound request logs or blocked domain metrics are transmitted back to Andar Studio.

### 5. Third-Party Websites & External Services

* **Embedded Web Rendering:** PrivBrowser renders web content using CefSharp (Chromium Embedded Framework). Web pages you visit may set cookies, run scripts, or store data according to their own privacy policies.
* **Search Engine Queries:** Address bar queries not entered as explicit URLs default to search engine endpoints (e.g., Google Search). These queries are subject to the privacy policy of the chosen search provider.

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in software architecture or legal compliance. Updated policy revisions will be published directly to the repository.
